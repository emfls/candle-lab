# Candle Lab: synthetic chart desk

A focused, Korean-first practice desk, not a brokerage. The owner delegated theme and implementation choices and authorized the existing GitHub Pages deployment.

Use an instrument-panel composition: compact masthead, terminal-style title, synthetic-only safety ribbon, connected account strip, expansive chart and a tactile decision console. Warm graphite, mint and coral, sharp grid lines, tabular numbers, purposeful labels. Three numbered steps make the workflow legible. No decorative market statistics or real-trading claims.

The existing engine, generated OHLC data, identifiers and all interactions remain unchanged. The noindex directive, relative URLs and caveats remain. No external artwork, dependency, API, tracking, account, trades or money. No financial promises. Responsive layout and visible keyboard focus are required. Compared with a poster-first landing page, a working desk puts the chart and decision within reach. A generic shared-card template was rejected because distinct thematic structure is the goal.
